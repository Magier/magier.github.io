Create a wide editorial illustration for a personal technology and agentic AI security blog.

**SUBJECT:** [INSERT TOPIC]

**CONCEPT:** [DESCRIBE THE IDEA OR METAPHOR]

### Visual identity

Japanese sumi-e ink-wash and traditional woodblock print inspired illustration, reinterpreted for contemporary technical subjects.

The aesthetic is quiet, contemplative, slightly mysterious, and gently whimsical. Combine traditional Japanese landscape painting with simple mechanical forms and abstract technical geometry.

Use expressive dry-brush strokes, imperfect hand-drawn lines, subtle ink bleeding, uneven pigment density, and tactile printmaking textures.

The illustration should feel handcrafted, atmospheric, and timeless—not digital, corporate, or futuristic.

### Color palette

**Dark mode:**
- Background: deep blue-black `#171922`
- Primary ink: warm off-white `#e5e2da`
- Accent: dusty periwinkle `#899de2`
- Optional secondary accent: muted clay `#c18e79`, used extremely sparingly

**Light mode:**
- Background: warm ivory `#f7f4ed`
- Primary ink: soft charcoal `#30313b`
- Accent: muted periwinkle `#899de2`
- Secondary ink: warm gray `#a9a49e`

Keep backgrounds flat and consistent for seamless integration into a website. No gradients or visible paper borders.

Use the accent color deliberately on selected focal elements, foliage, celestial bodies, or small mechanical details. It should be clearly visible without dominating the composition.

### Composition

Create a wide, cinematic landscape composition, approximately 2:1.

The central subject should be recognizable at small sizes, with a clear silhouette and restrained detail.

Integrate the subject organically into a Japanese-inspired landscape using elements such as:
- Windswept pine trees, delicate blossoms, climbing vines
- Weathered rocks, distant mountains, stepping stones
- Sparse clouds, a textured moon, drifting leaves
- Architectural structures made from stone, timber, or simple geometric forms

Choose only the elements that support the concept. Do not include all of them by default.

Technical objects should feel like natural inhabitants of this world rather than pasted-on diagrams.

Balance detailed focal areas with generous negative space. Allow the landscape and subject to interact through overlapping forms, vegetation, shared brush textures, and atmospheric perspective.

### Character and storytelling

When depicting AI agents, use small, expressive humanoid robots with rounded mechanical heads, simple circular eyes, understated joints, and subtle personality.

Robots should feel curious, thoughtful, and slightly strange—never aggressive or overly cute.

Use visual metaphors to communicate the topic without relying on labels, symbols, or explanatory diagrams.

The scene should tell a small, intriguing story.

### Constraints

No text, lettering, logos, watermarks, UI elements, glowing circuits, neon, glossy surfaces, 3D rendering, photorealism, or corporate cybersecurity imagery.

Avoid overly polished vector art, excessive detail, perfect symmetry, and generic sci-fi aesthetics.

Do not automatically reuse the same robot, enclosure, moon, or landscape arrangement. Each illustration needs its own composition while retaining the same artistic identity.

**Final impression:** A contemporary technical idea illustrated by a traditional Japanese ink artist with a subtle fascination for machines. Calm, poetic, intelligent, and distinctive.
